# Personal Action Plan: Huberman Protocols Implementation

A realistic, phased plan to integrate science-based protocols into daily life. Each phase builds on the last. Do NOT try everything at once -- that's the fastest way to quit.

---

## Phase 1: The Non-Negotiables (Weeks 1-2)

These three habits give the highest return for the least effort. Nail these before adding anything else.

### 1. Morning Sunlight -- Day 1

- [ ] Go outside within 30-60 min of waking
- [ ] 5-10 min on sunny days, 15 min on overcast days
- [ ] No sunglasses, no window -- direct outdoor light
- [ ] If it's still dark when you wake, turn on bright indoor lights and get outside once the sun is up

**Why this first:** It fixes your circadian clock, which cascades into better sleep, energy, mood, and focus. Everything else works better when your clock is set.

### 2. Fix Your Sleep Window -- Day 1

- [ ] Pick a consistent bedtime and wake time (e.g., 10:30 PM / 6:30 AM)
- [ ] Stick to it 7 days a week (yes, weekends too)
- [ ] Make the room COLD (65-67F), DARK (blackout curtains or eye mask), and QUIET
- [ ] Dim lights after sunset -- no bright overhead lighting in the evening

**Why this second:** Sleep is the single biggest lever for everything -- focus, mood, recovery, motivation. A bad sleep schedule undermines every other protocol.

### 3. Delay Caffeine -- Day 1

- [ ] Wait 90-120 minutes after waking for your first coffee/tea
- [ ] Drink water first (2 glasses, optionally add pinch of salt + lemon)
- [ ] Cut all caffeine by noon

**Why this third:** Costs nothing, requires no new habit -- just shifting an existing one. Prevents afternoon crashes and protects sleep.

### Phase 1 Daily Schedule

```
6:30 AM   Wake up, drink water with salt
6:45 AM   Go outside for sunlight (10 min)
8:30 AM   First caffeine
12:00 PM  Caffeine cutoff
10:30 PM  Lights out, cool dark room
```

**Success criteria:** Do this consistently for 2 weeks. You should notice improved energy and sleep quality before moving on.

## Phase 2: Stress & Recovery Tools (Weeks 3-4)

Now add tools that manage stress and recharge your brain mid-day.

### 4. Physiological Sigh -- Use As Needed

- [ ] Practice the technique: double inhale (nose) + long exhale (mouth)
- [ ] Use it whenever you feel stressed, anxious, or overwhelmed
- [ ] Use it between sets if you exercise
- [ ] Takes 10-30 seconds, works immediately

### 5. NSDR (Non-Sleep Deep Rest) -- Daily

- [ ] Pick one 10-20 min guided NSDR audio (search "Huberman NSDR" on YouTube/Spotify)
- [ ] Do it once per day, ideally early-to-mid afternoon (1-3 PM)
- [ ] Lie down, eyes closed, follow the audio
- [ ] Use it to beat the afternoon slump instead of reaching for more caffeine

### Phase 2 Addition to Daily Schedule

```
1:00-3:00 PM  NSDR session (10-20 min)
As needed      Physiological sigh for acute stress
```

**Success criteria:** NSDR becomes a daily habit. You use the physiological sigh reflexively when stressed.

## Phase 3: Structured Work & Movement (Weeks 5-8)

Now layer in the productivity and exercise framework.

### 6. 90-Minute Deep Work Blocks

- [ ] Schedule your first 90-min block in the morning (after caffeine kicks in)
- [ ] Phone in another room, notifications off
- [ ] Accept the first 10-15 min will feel hard -- this is normal (warm-up period)
- [ ] Take a real break after (walk, NSDR, light movement -- NOT social media)
- [ ] Add a second block in the afternoon if needed (max 2-3 per day)

### 7. Start Exercising (Simplified Starter Version)

Don't jump into Huberman's full 7-day split. Start here:

**Week 5-6: Build the habit (3 days/week)**

| Day | What | Duration |
|-----|------|----------|
| Mon | Walk/jog at conversational pace (Zone 2) | 30 min |
| Wed | Bodyweight strength (push-ups, squats, rows, planks) | 30 min |
| Fri | Walk/jog at conversational pace (Zone 2) | 30 min |

**Week 7-8: Expand (4-5 days/week)**

| Day | What | Duration |
|-----|------|----------|
| Sun | Zone 2 cardio (jog, bike, swim) | 45-60 min |
| Mon | Legs (gym or bodyweight) | 45 min |
| Wed | Upper body (gym or bodyweight) | 45 min |
| Fri | HIIT (sprints: 20-30 sec on, rest, 8 rounds) | 20 min |

**Timing tip:** Exercise near the end of your fasting window (late morning) if possible.

### Phase 3 Updated Schedule

```
6:30 AM    Wake, hydrate, sunlight
8:30 AM    Caffeine
9:00 AM    Deep work block 1 (90 min)
10:30 AM   Break / exercise
12:00 PM   First meal

5:30 PM    Sunset viewing (5 min)
7:30 PM    Dinner (include starchy carbs)
10:30 PM   Sleep
```

**Success criteria:** You're exercising 3+ days/week and completing at least one focused 90-min work block daily.

## Phase 4: Nutrition Timing (Weeks 9-10)

### 8. Time-Restricted Eating

- [ ] Stop eating 2-3 hours before bed (non-negotiable -- start here)
- [ ] Gradually push your first meal later (target: 6-8 hours after waking)
- [ ] Start with a 12-hour eating window, then narrow to 10, then 8
- [ ] First meal: protein + vegetables, low carb (keeps you alert)
- [ ] Last meal: include starchy carbs (rice, potatoes) to support sleep

**Don't rush this.** Shift your eating window by ~1 hour every few days until you reach your target.

### 9. Evening Sunlight Viewing

- [ ] Spend 5-10 min outside in late afternoon/sunset light
- [ ] Protects your circadian rhythm from artificial evening light

## Phase 5: Optimization (Weeks 11+)

Only add these once Phases 1-4 are solid habits.

### 10. Cold Exposure (Optional but Powerful)

- [ ] Start with 30 sec of cold water at the end of your shower
- [ ] Gradually increase to 1-3 min
- [ ] Target: 11 min total cold exposure per week across 2-4 sessions
- [ ] Do it in the morning for a dopamine boost lasting 2-4 hours
- [ ] End on cold -- don't warm up artificially afterward

### 11. Sleep Supplements (Only If Needed)

Try one at a time, one week each:

1. Magnesium L-Threonate (140 mg) -- 30 min before bed
2. If needed, add Apigenin (50 mg)
3. If needed, add L-Theanine (100-300 mg)

### 12. Foundational Supplements (Optional)

- Omega-3 fish oil (~2g EPA/day)
- Vitamin D3 (5,000 IU/day, adjust with bloodwork)
- Creatine (5g/day)

### 13. Full Fitness Protocol

Graduate to Huberman's full 7-day split (see huberman-protocols.md).

### 14. Dopamine Management

- [ ] Stop stacking dopamine triggers (caffeine + music + phone before work)
- [ ] Intermittently skip rewards to keep motivation circuits sharp
- [ ] Reframe effort as the reward, not just the outcome

## Quick Rules to Live By

1. **Morning sunlight is non-negotiable** -- do it every single day
2. **Sleep is the foundation** -- protect it above all else
3. **Add one thing at a time** -- if you try to change everything at once, you'll change nothing
4. **Consistency beats intensity** -- 80% adherence over months beats 100% for two weeks
5. **Track what matters** -- note your sleep quality, energy, and focus daily (even just 1-10 scores)
6. **Get bloodwork** -- test twice per year to see what's actually happening inside

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Doing too much at once.** Pick Phase 1. Do it for 2 weeks. Then add Phase 2.
- **Skipping sleep optimization.** Nothing else works well on bad sleep.
- **Taking supplements before fixing behaviors.** Supplements are the last 5%, not the first.
- **Being rigid instead of consistent.** Miss a day? Just do it tomorrow. Don't spiral.
- **Comparing to Huberman's exact routine.** He's been doing this 20+ years. Adapt the principles to YOUR life.
